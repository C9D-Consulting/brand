FINDING 01

The AI roadmap is three decisions pretending to be one.

Most portfolio companies split AI adoption into a tooling decision, a staffing decision and a governance decision. The split feels analytical. It is the opposite.

The three interact, and treating them apart is how pilot programs stall at the demo.

Decide once, with the interactions visible.
