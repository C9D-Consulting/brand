## Brief · Where rollouts actually fail

| Term | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Shape | Fixed scope, fixed fee, one engagement at a time |
| Duration | 6 to 10 weeks, sized in Assess |
| For | Private-equity portfolio companies and growth-stage teams consolidating onto one workspace |
| Moving from | Jira, Trello, spreadsheets, email and shared inboxes |
| Led by | Brandon Wilburn, Principal |

ClickUp configures quickly, and most teams can stand up a workspace in a week. That is rarely where adoption breaks. It breaks underneath: no one owns the hierarchy, intake bypasses the system, automations pile up without owners, and leadership keeps asking for status in a spreadsheet because the dashboards cannot be trusted.

We treat ClickUp as the surface and the operating model as the work. Every decision about Spaces, Folders, Lists, statuses and fields is written down with an owner, so the next leader inherits **a playbook, not a mess.**

## Method · Four Phases

| Phase | Window | Work |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Assess | Weeks 1 to 2 | Map workflows, intake paths, reporting needs and the tools being retired. |
| Design | Weeks 2 to 4 | Workspace hierarchy, statuses, custom fields, permissions and governance rules, captured as a short operating playbook. |
| Adopt | Weeks 4 to 8 | One pilot team first, measured. Then rollout in waves with role-based training and named champions. |
| Sustain | Through day 90 | Adoption metrics, a 30/60/90 review, and a named governance owner on the client side. |

## Extensions · Beyond the core engagement

#### Extension A · Automation and AI

Intake triage, status rollups, SLA and handoff rules, and AI-assisted summaries for leadership, using ClickUp Automations and ClickUp Brain. Each automation is documented so the team can run it after we leave.

#### Extension B · Systems Integration

ClickUp connected to the systems already in place: CRM, Slack, GitHub, Google Workspace and finance tools. We decide the system of record for each object first, then build.
