# AI enablement readout

## 1 · Where the work stands

What the diagnostic found, and the three decisions it forces before the next board meeting.

### FINDING 01 · The AI roadmap is three decisions pretending to be one.

Most portfolio companies split AI adoption into a tooling decision, a staffing decision and a governance decision. The split feels analytical. It is the opposite.

The three interact, and treating them apart is how *pilot programs* stall at the demo.

> Decide once, with the interactions visible.

Notes: Open with the pattern, not the client. Pause after the second paragraph.

### Observer versus operator

#### THE OBSERVER

Advises from outside the company. Accountability ends with the SOW.

- Frameworks drawn from watching many firms
- Recommendations that stop at the deck

#### THE OPERATOR

Has carried the work inside the company, long past any engagement.

- Judgment drawn from shipping and running systems
- Recommendations that arrive as a playbook
