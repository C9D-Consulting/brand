# C9D Consulting Brand — The Dossier

The brand surface is a declassified document, not a marketing site. Every artifact is a piece of operating evidence. The reader is a CEO, board member, CFO, or incoming engineering executive who needs to verify the work before trusting it. Polished marketing decoration breaks the contract; document-grade composition keeps it.

This skill operates on the locked decisions from the original brand-build session (chat 6e24e7a3-77e3-4a61-9959-eb2e64479477), refined through v2.0 (broader portfolio reframe), v2.1 (advising-vs-operating credential distinction), v2.2 (observers vocabulary lock + SOW-boundedness axis), and v2.3 (AI-forward cornerstone reframe). Where a locked decision was not fully captured in this skill's source extraction, the relevant reference file flags the gap.

## When this skill loads

Whenever any of these surfaces is in scope, load this skill before producing the artifact:

- C9D Consulting deck (any format)
- C9D Consulting proposal, SOW, engagement letter
- C9D Consulting Word document (memo, findings, playbook, case write-up)
- C9D Consulting spreadsheet (analysis, decision register, risk register, qualification grid)
- C9D Consulting web page (external site, internal tool, status page)
- C9D Consulting email (signature, outbound, transactional)
- Anything that names an engagement (E-01, E-02, E-03, R-01)
- Anything labeled "A practice of Brandon Wilburn"

If a sibling brand is in scope instead (Squidward, Coordinated App, Coordinated Kids, Coordinated Social, C9D Holdings), do NOT use this skill. Squidward, Coordinated App, etc. have their own brand systems.

If brandonwilburn.pro or TechieBrandon.com is in scope, these are federated siblings within the C9D Consulting visual system (Posture B per §9.1). The visual system applies; the voice and positioning are property-specific.

## How to use this skill

1. **Read `references/foundation.md` first.** It carries the locked positioning (AI Strategy Advisory frame, post-shipping AI strategy POD, the contrarian belief, the three RTBs, the primary alternative). Every other file is downstream.

2. **Load `references/visual-system.md`** for color tokens, typography, logo lockups, and spatial composition. The 70/22/6/2 composition ratio is load-bearing.

3. **Load `references/voice-library.md`** for the tagline system (primary / alternate / campaign / closing line), owned vocabulary, refuse list, naming conventions, voice principles.

4. **Load `references/engagement-architecture.md`** if the work touches services, pricing, qualification, or delivery.

5. **Load the relevant `templates/*-spec.md`** for the output format. NOTE: each template file is currently a **PROPOSAL** — a reasonable application of The Dossier to that format, not a locked source decision. Treat as starting point, not as system, until reviewed with Brandon.

6. **Use `assets/tokens.css` or `assets/tokens.json`** if producing code, web, or design-tool deliverables.

## Non-negotiable invariants — v2.0

These rules apply to every C9D Consulting artifact. Derived from locked source decisions and updated for v2.0 positioning.

1. **AI Strategy & Enablement is the cornerstone frame, delivered as Operator Advisory & Strategy (v2.3).** Site subtitles, hero copy, and lead surfaces open with "AI Strategy & Enablement." The "Operator Advisory & Strategy" framing follows as the qualifier that separates the practice from the mass market of AI-strategy observers. Not "fractional CTO." Not "AI Strategy Advisory alone" (v1.x, retired — too narrow). Not "generic Operator Advisory & Strategy" (v2.0–v2.2, retired — buried the AI market pull). v2.3 leads with AI market pull, backs it with operator credential differentiation.

2. **POD: AI Strategy & Enablement, delivered by an operator across cycles.** Two-part POD, both parts required. Part A (what): AI Strategy & Enablement is the leading offering — market pull is real, work is defensible. Part B (why C9D and not the mass): nearly two decades of operator experience across enterprise software, technology & business strategy, governance, compliance, cybersecurity, mobile, SaaS, machine learning, and new product introduction is what makes the AI work survive real diligence. Neither part alone is the POD; both together are.

3. **Three RTBs at brandonwilburn.pro carry the proof spine.** Luma for Landslide (depth) / Seven-Dimension Review + DORA (transferability) / Spirent $600M → $1.3B Keysight acquisition (stakes). Nothing is claimed in copy that one of the three cannot prove — or that operator experience across cycles cannot defend.

4. **Full name only in body copy.** Always "C9D Consulting." Never "C9D" alone. Single exception: U-06 favicon.

5. **Attribution required.** "A practice of Brandon Wilburn" on every major surface.

6. **Tagline system is locked at four constructs (v2.3):**
   - Primary: "The playbook, not the deck."
   - Alternate: "Operator, not observer." (v2.3 lock; replaces the v2.0 "Decades in the seat" draft and the v1.x "We've shipped what we recommend")
   - Campaign: "Judgment across cycles." (v2.0 replaces "AI strategy that survives the next round")
   - Closing line (NOT a tagline): "Coordinated, not improvised."

7. **70/22/6/2 composition ratio.**

8. **Sodium amber indexes the proof.**

9. **Classified red is rationed.** Refusals only.

10. **Refused vocabulary is refused.** Even when the client uses it.

11. **Em-dashes are watched.**

12. **Sequential, not parallel.** One engagement at a time.

13. **The retainer is the exit pattern.** R-01 does not start cold.

14. **Voice posture: Sage anchor + Creator secondary.** Reference patterns: Patrick Collison, Stripe's writing, Ben Horowitz. Diagnose, don't moralize.

15. **The Dossier is dark by default.** Light mode is constrained-use only (print artifacts).

16. **Refuse engagement shapes, not domains (v2.0).** Refuse hourly, pure implementation, roadmap decks, outcome guarantees on undelivered work, and approaches the operator cannot defend from prior experience. Do NOT refuse based on vertical or industry — that constraint retired in v2.0.

17. **Federated relationship with brandonwilburn.pro (v2.0 Option C).** C9D Consulting and brandonwilburn.pro intentionally offer complementary modes of engagement in parallel. brandonwilburn.pro positions Brandon Wilburn's candidacy for full-time VP Engineering / Head of AI operator roles ("selective advisory only"). c9d.consulting positions the Operator Advisory & Strategy practice. Both are simultaneously offered; a visitor to either understands they are seeing one mode of possible engagement. The "Q3 2026 · 01 SLOT OPEN" indicator on c9d.consulting reflects this sequential-availability discipline — engagements are selective, not exclusive to Brandon's calendar. Neither property should imply the other is secondary.

18. **Boundary: no brandonwilburn.pro edits from this skill (v2.0).** brandonwilburn.pro is out of scope. This skill does not propose, produce, or coordinate updates to brandonwilburn.pro copy, structure, or positioning. Alignment between the two properties is intentional per invariant 17, not enforced through cross-editing. Brandon owns brandonwilburn.pro decisions separately.

19. **Deal-value framing on Spirent → Keysight (v2.0).** The credential is carried by **scope** (four concurrent strategic initiatives) and **premium** (fifteen percent over Viavi's competing bid). Deal value has been intentionally deprioritized in all copy — the $1.3B close figure is public record but is not used in positioning, hero copy, case cards, or credential footers. Master brand (brandonwilburn.pro) follows the same convention. The $600M reference to Spirent's size (as contextualizing the "test and assurance company" scale) is acceptable when used non-comparatively; the "$600M → $1.3B" trajectory framing has been retired.

20. **Advising vs operating credential distinction (v2.2).** The primary alternative is *"the advisor whose credential is observing, not operating."* Two axes distinguish the two credentials: **(a) position** — outside the company vs inside the company, and **(b) accountability duration** — SOW-bounded vs long-past-any-engagement. The Dossier voice reasons from the operating credential; the advising credential is conceded as real but different in kind. **Vocabulary lock:** use **"observers"** (never "watchers") when referring to the class of outside advisors; "observers" is more accurate and less pejorative. The distinction should be drawn structurally, not moralistically — advisors are not attacked, they are named as a different tool for a different job.

21. **AI-forward architecture (v2.3).** AI Strategy & Enablement is the cornerstone offering because it is the hottest advisory market and where growth-stage buyers are actively searching. The broader capability portfolio serves two functions in messaging: **(a) support** — the domains adjacent to AI (governance, compliance, cybersecurity, IP, product, capital events) that reinforce AI work in the specific engagement, and **(b) distinction** — the multi-cycle operator credential that separates the practice from the mass market of AI-strategy observers. Frame surfaces lead with AI; portfolio surfaces follow. **The engagement architecture stays domain-neutral** (Decision Advisory, Posture & Diligence Review, Operating Partner Retainer) because AI is the DOMAIN, not the SHAPE — most engagements are AI decisions but the engagement shape is portable to non-AI decisions when they arise. Lead with AI in visible copy; keep the engagement architecture broad in the commercial structure.
