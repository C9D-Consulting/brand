## Taglines (three-layer system) + closing line

**[locked v2.0]** Three taglines plus one closing line. Roles distinct; do not substitute. **Alternate and campaign taglines updated in v2.0** to reflect the broader Operator Advisory & Strategy frame; primary tagline and closing line unchanged.

### Primary tagline (unchanged)

> **"The playbook, not the deck."**

- **Role:** Default tagline. Hero copy, sign-offs, brand-recognition placements.
- **Why still locked in v2.0:** The primary tagline works identically for AI strategy work, PE advisory, fractional CTO, or ops consulting — the "playbook vs deck" antagonism is scope-neutral.

### Alternate tagline (v2.3 — locked)

> **"Operator, not observer."**

- **Role:** Long-form / sub-hero variant. The credential distinction compressed to three words. Speakable (repeats well in intro calls, outbound, podcast intros); scannable (works in visual chrome, deck footers, email signatures).
- **Why locked v2.3:** Replaces "Decades in the seat" (v2.0 draft, never final) which conflated tenure with credential. Also replaces "We've shipped what we recommend" (v1.x) which was scope-restrictive. "Operator, not observer" carries the v2.2 credential distinction (position + accountability-duration axes) and reinforces the vocabulary lock on "observers" from invariant 20. Rhythmically matches the manifesto's "Not observed. Operated." closer.
- **Adversarial note:** Loses the "across cycles" claim that "Decades in the seat" carried. Trade accepted: the terse three-word form gets repeated, which is what an alternate tagline needs to do. The multi-cycle claim now lives in the campaign tagline ("Judgment across cycles.") and in the manifesto arc.

### Shelf taglines (retired to secondary use, still deployable)

- **"We've shipped what we recommend."** (v1.x alternate) — still true, still deployable in AI-specific contexts. Not the primary alternate.
- **"Decades in the seat."** (v2.0 draft) — never fully locked; considered and set aside in favor of "Operator, not observer." Still available for shelf use if the multi-cycle-tenure claim needs a compact form.

### Campaign tagline (v2.0 — updated)

> **"Judgment across cycles."**

- **Role:** Campaign signature for materials emphasizing the multi-cycle credential. Board-facing, PE-facing, capital-events copy.
- **Why locked v2.0:** Replaces "AI strategy that survives the next round" (v1.x campaign), which was AI-specific and scope-restrictive. "Judgment across cycles" is the compression of the v2.0 POD.

### Closing line — NOT the tagline (unchanged)

> **"Coordinated, not improvised."**

- **Role:** Closing-line signature for manifesto-grade documents, footers, campaign signatures.
- **Why still locked:** Numeronym-discovery enabler. Brandon's own voice. Untouched in v2.0.
- **Substitution:** Never. The phrase is locked.

**Important:** The closing line is a separate construct from the primary tagline. Do not conflate.

## Four voice principles (consistent across all contexts)

**[locked]**

1. **Evidence-first.** Claims are followed by proof artifacts. If a claim cannot be backed by an RTB or a documented case, it does not appear in copy.

2. **Plain-named-precision.** Name things specifically (Windsurf, Devin, DORA, Keysight, Viavi) rather than abstractly (AI tooling, productivity metrics, acquirers). Plain words; specific names.

3. **Concede-first.** Acknowledge what's true in the opposing position before naming what's wrong.

4. **Diagnose-not-moralize.** Describe patterns and costs. Do not moralize about failures or the industry. Critical, but not cynical.
